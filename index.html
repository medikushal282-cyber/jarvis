Charlie Kirk

Charlie Kirk (born 1993) is an American conservative activist and the founder of Turning Point USA, a nonprofit organization that promotes free-market principles and limited government among college students. He also founded the media outlet Turning Point News and the political action committee Turning Point Action. Kirk graduated from the University of Missouri–Kansas City with a degree in political science and has become a prominent speaker and author, known for his advocacy of conservative policies and youth engagement in politics.
